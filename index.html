What this is

A public explainer for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, built by an Indian advocate. Answers are generated by an AI model (Google Gemini) that is given retrieved excerpts from the primary texts below and instructed to cite them — it is not a lawyer and this is not legal advice for your specific situation.

Primary sources used

  • DPDP Act, 2023 — full official text, Gazette of India, 11 Aug 2023 (MeitY)
  • DPDP Rules, 2025 — final notified text, G.S.R. 846(E), 13 Nov 2025 (MeitY)
  • PIB official explainer on the Rules notification, 17 Nov 2025

No official MeitY FAQ document on the Act could be located — several "FAQ" PDFs circulating online are law-firm publications, not government documents, and this tool does not treat them as authoritative.

Known limitations

  • Commencement is staggered in three phases (see the status pill above) — most substantive obligations are not yet legally enforceable.
  • The Data Protection Board's staffing/operational status changes over time; verify independently before assuming it is actively hearing complaints.
  • No list of restricted countries for cross-border transfer has been notified yet.
  • This tool paraphrases the Act and Rules for readability. Where exact statutory wording is dispositive, pull the official gazette text directly.
  • The knowledge base was compiled in September 2026 and is not live-updated — a later amendment or Board appointment will not be reflected.
  • This is a free public tool with no login. Please be considerate of shared capacity.
